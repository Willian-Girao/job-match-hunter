🚀 Senior AI Platform Engineer - Beacon Robotics
Eindhoven / remote within the EU | Full-time

We build autonomy software for warehouse robots, but this opening is not a research-scientist role. The team behind the models needs a platform that makes training repeatable, observable and boring—in the best possible way. You would sit between ML researchers, infrastructure engineers and data teams.

What you would take ownership of
You will evolve our GPU training environment, standardize how experiments are launched, and improve the path from a research repository to a production-ready training job. Today that path uses containerized Python workloads, Kubernetes, Terraform-managed infrastructure and CI/CD in GitHub Actions. Some pipelines land training data in BigQuery; operational metadata is stored in PostgreSQL. You do not need to be a database specialist, but you do need to be comfortable diagnosing data-flow problems.

For this role, production Python and Kubernetes experience are non-negotiable. We also expect solid hands-on experience with Docker, infrastructure-as-code (Terraform or an equivalent tool), and at least one major cloud platform (GCP, AWS or Azure). You should have spent 5+ years in software, ML engineering, data engineering or a closely related production engineering role, including at least 2 years supporting ML/AI workloads in production.

You will work directly with researchers who use PyTorch. You don't have to be a modeling specialist, but you should understand enough of a training loop and GPU workload to troubleshoot platform issues. Experience with distributed data or messaging systems is useful; Kafka or Spark would be a plus. Ray experience gets bonus points, and familiarity with NVIDIA Triton is nice to have rather than required. ✨

We normally look for a bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. A master's degree is a plus, not a requirement.

The team communicates in English every day, so professional fluency is required. Dutch is helpful for local collaboration but entirely optional. 🇳🇱

You might enjoy this role if you like removing friction for other engineers, writing clear platform interfaces and chasing down failures that span application code, orchestration and cloud infrastructure.

Benefits: flexible hours, home-office budget, 30 days' leave and an annual learning allowance. 🌍
