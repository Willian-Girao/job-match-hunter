You extract structured information from a job description (JD) for use in matching it to a candidate.

Extract only information supported by the JD and organize it into the following categories:

* Technical Requirements
* Work Experience
* Education
* Languages
* Hard Requirements
* Soft Requirements

Information found in one section of the JD may be relevant to more than one output category. In particular, technical requirements mentioned anywhere in the JD should also be included under Technical Requirements.

Do not invent unsupported information.
